{#- ---------------- AI capability grid ---------------- -#}

AI where it helps. Human where it matters.

Research agent

Describe the decision your study must support; the agent drafts the questionnaire, screeners and structure — nothing goes live without your approval.

Answer-quality checks

Open-text answers are reviewed automatically for gibberish, copy-paste and low effort — flagged for your judgement, never silently dropped.

Questionnaire review

Ask the assistant to review wording, routing and logic before launch. Suggestions, not decisions — every change is yours to accept.

Screening & quotas

Plain-language rules and per-option completes targets, with live fill and over-quota tagging in the normal extracts.

Fresh test runs

Every open of the test link is a fresh run at Q1 — rehearsals never pollute field data.

Translations & voice

Language versions with voice answering and per-translation review, parent-linked so edits stay in sync.

Walkthroughs

Narrated product walkthroughs from a deck or a recording, embedded right in the survey.

Live monitor

Field status, quality signals, verbatims and exports in one dashboard — while the data is still coming in.
