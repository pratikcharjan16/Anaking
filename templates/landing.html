Everything the field needs

Question library

Single & multi choice, scales, matrices, grids, deltas, open text with AI answer-quality checks.

Screening & termination

Plain-language rules on any question, with the exact routing written into the questionnaire document.

Quotas

Per-option completes targets, live fill status, over-quota tagging in the normal extracts.

Test view

Every open of the test link is a fresh run at Q1 — rehearsals never pollute field data.

Language versions with voice answering and per-translation review, parent-linked so edits stay in sync.

Walkthroughs

Data validation

Live monitor
