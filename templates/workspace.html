THE RESEARCH JOURNEY

From a good question to a clear decision.

  1. Design

    Define objectives, audience and questionnaire. Keep advanced settings tucked away until needed.

  2. Test & translate

    Run readiness checks, manually test every route and review translations with a native speaker.

  3. Review & publish

    Approve an exact draft. Publishing locks a version; subsequent edits stay in draft.

  4. Monitor & understand

    Keep real and test data separate. Inspect answered bases and review original verbatims.

Checks are not a certification of research validity or legal compliance. Automated guidance uses rules; model assistance is optional and requires configuration.
